Structural Analysis

Thesis Statement

Through emotional anecdotes, statistical evidence, and examples of social change, the author argues that dogs have become important family members in South Korea, helping people cope with loneliness while transforming the country's culture, economy, and attitudes toward animals.

The deceased lay wrapped in a cotton blanket，surrounded by white roses and hydrangea，angelic figurines and lit candles and incense. A wall-mounted screen displayed photographs of him. His 71-year-old companion, Kim Seon-ae, convulsed with tears as she bid farewell, caressing his head and face. Next door, young uniformed morticians prepared for his cremation.
The elaborate and emotional ritual was for a white poodle named Dalkong, who was nestled in a willow basket with his eyes still open.
“He was like a virus that infected me with happiness," said Ms. Kim, who had lived with Dalkong for 13 years until hesuccumbed to heart disease. “We were family.”

Essay Outline

  • #Sec. 1: “He was like a virus that infected me with happiness. We were family.”

    • The article begins with the funeral of Dalkong to demonstrate the profound emotional attachment between South Koreans and their dogs.

  • #Sec. 2: “More South Koreans are choosing to stay single, childless or both.”; “More than two-fifths of all households in the nation now consist of only one person.”

    • The author explains that demographic changes and social isolation have increased the demand for companionship in South Korea.

  • #Sec. 3: “In this age of mistrust and loneliness, dogs show you what unconditional love is.”; “Dogs follow you like you are the center of the universe.”

    • Many South Koreans view dogs as reliable sources of unconditional love and emotional support.

  • #Sec. 4: “Hospitals and shops catering to pets have become ubiquitous, while childbirth clinics have all but disappeared.”

    • As dogs become family members, South Korea's social and economic landscape has changed dramatically.

  • #Sec. 5: “I had come to accept Liam as ‘my grandson.”

    • Dogs have become deeply integrated into family relationships and social activities.

  • #Sec. 6: “They don't think of replacing them but think of what the problem is and what they can do to fix it.”

    • The growing status of dogs as family members has led to changes in both the pet industry and animal welfare policies.

  • #Sec. 7: “No matter how old the dog is when it dies, it is still a child to its human family.”

    • The article concludes by showing that elaborate pet funerals symbolize the ultimate expression of dogs' family status.

Coherence & Conhension

#Sec. 1

Coherence

The funeral scene immediately introduces the article's central argument that dogs are no longer viewed as ordinary pets. By opening with an emotional farewell, the author establishes a strong foundation for the later discussion about companionship, loneliness, and changing family structures.

Cohension

The statement “We were family” connects closely with later descriptions such as “Dogs are family members that get splurged on” and “Now they treat them like family members.” These repeated references to family create a consistent theme that links the introduction to the rest of the article.

#Sec. 2

Coherence

After presenting an emotional example, the author broadens the discussion to explain the social context behind this phenomenon. The statistics help readers understand that Dalkong's story reflects a larger trend rather than an isolated case

Cohension

This section connects with the previous funeral story by explaining why people develop such strong attachments to dogs. The idea of loneliness is later echoed in the quote “dogs show you what unconditional love is,” creating a clear transition to the next section.

#Sec. 3

Coherence

This section logically follows the discussion of loneliness by explaining how dogs fulfill emotional needs. Instead of merely presenting statistics, the author provides personal experiences to demonstrate the benefits of companionship.

Cohension

The phrase “age of mistrust and loneliness” directly links back to the previous section's discussion of single-person households. It also prepares readers for the later examples of owners treating dogs like children and family members.

#Sec. 4

Coherence

After showing the emotional significance of dogs, the author expands the discussion to broader societal impacts. This shift demonstrates that changing human-dog relationships affect not only individuals but also businesses and communities.

Cohension

The quote “Dogs are family members that get splurged on” reinforces the earlier idea that dogs are treated like family. This repeated concept helps connect personal feelings with economic behaviors.

#Sec. 5

Coherence

This section deepens the argument by showing how dogs have become integrated into family structures. The examples illustrate that companionship has evolved into genuine familial relationships.

Cohension

The idea of dogs as children connects with the earlier references to unconditional love and family membership. It also supports the later discussion about pet funerals, where owners continue treating dogs like family even after death.

#Sec. 6

Coherence

The author moves from family relationships to social responsibility, demonstrating how people's affection for dogs influences business practices and legislation. This broadens the article's scope from private experiences to public change.

Cohension

The phrase “family members” echoes similar expressions used throughout the article. It also links to examples of pet services and welfare reforms, showing how emotional attachment translates into practical action.

#Sec. 7

Coherence

The conclusion returns to the funeral theme introduced at the beginning of the article. This circular structure reinforces the author's main argument and provides a satisfying sense of closure.

Cohension

The final statement that a dog remains “a child to its human family” directly echoes earlier descriptions such as “Liam is like a child to me” and “We were family.” These repeated ideas tie the entire article together and strengthen its overall unity.
