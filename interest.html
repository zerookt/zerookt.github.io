What's My Hobby?

Besides serving as a college student, I am also a big fan of KPOP, and TREASURE is my favorite KPOP group.

TREASURE is a South Korean boy group under YG Entertainment. Since their debut in 2020, they have gained popularity both in Korea and globally because of their strong performances, diverse music styles, and engaging personalities. The group is currently consists of ten members and continues to attract a large fanbase around the world.

Before their debut, all members participated in a survival program called "YG Treasure Box." The show was created to select trainees who would success and become one of the members of YG Entertainment’s next boy group. Throughout the competition, the trainees demonstrated their singing, dancing, and performance abilities while competing for a debut opportunity. After months of evaluations and challenges, the final members were chosen, and TREASURE officially made their debut on August 7, 2020.

TREASURE’s debut song, “BOY,” introduced the group with a powerful concept and energetic performance. The song highlighted the members’ youthful image and showcased their talent in both singing and dancing. Following their debut, TREASURE released many catching songs that explored different musical styles, ranging from energetic dance tracks to emotional ballads.

In addition to their music, TREASURE is well known for its variety content called "TREASURE MAP." This series allows fans to see the members’ personalities beyond the stage. The episodes feature games, challenges, travel activities, and team competitions, creating many memorable and humorous moments. Through this content, fans can better understand the members’ friendships and teamwork, which has become one of the reasons for the group’s popularity. As a fan who started liking TREASURE through TREASURE MAP, I have rewatched all the episodes for over three times, everytime when I watched, I always find pleasure on them.

My favorite TREASURE song is “SLOWMOTION.” Though members love HIPHOP genre so much, I personally love the soft, gentle song better. This song has a warm and emotional atmosphere, and therefore everytime when I listened to this song I feel heart-warming and embraced. Additionally, the live atmosphere was so touching when I heard this song at TREASURE's world tour held three years ago. The impressive emotion was unforgettable that I always rank this song at an irreplaceable first place.

Among all the members, my favorite member is Park Jeongwoo. As one of the group’s main vocalists, he is recognized for his powerful voice and emotional singing style. I always feel calm when I heard his voice, especially when I have to concentrate, I always play the playlist that contain all his covered songs. Beside his vocal ability, I am also addicted to his cheerful and energetic personality. In TREASURE MAP, he is always the one who hypes and cheer members up with his humorous performances, and I can always receive great energy from him. With his great personality and incredible vocal skill, Park Jeongwoo is always said to have a great potential to grab fans' hearts, and I look forward to it.

In conclusion, TREASURE has developed into one of the most promising K-pop groups of their generation. Through their music, performances, and variety content, they have built a strong connection with fans worldwide. So far I have been liking them at the fourth year, with their companionship of heart-warming songs and vivid personalities, I always believe more and more people would observe this "treasure" and make their efforts deserve.
